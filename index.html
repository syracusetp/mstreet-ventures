Thesis

We target 5% ownership at Pre-seed and Seed with valuations of $1M–$5M, syndicating deals into $40k–$150k checks across 5–15 people.

We support ideas that violate normative ways of thinking, are steeped in scientific evidence, and provide a clear vision of the world to come — especially from diverse founders in the US and across Africa, and scientists commercializing breakthrough research.
